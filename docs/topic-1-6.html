Unit training and operational qualification

Unit training connects prior learning to performance in a particular operational setting. Its output depends on training opportunities, instructors, assessment and progression—not just the number of trainees entering.

Where the bottleneck can arise

  • Operational staffing pressure can reduce the staff available to instruct.
  • Limited instructor availability can constrain training throughput.
  • Slower qualification delays the replenishment of the operational workforce.

This feedback chain is an Atlas synthesis of mechanisms discussed in the study, not a separately quantified feedback model.

Source basis and evidence status

F: sections 2.3 and 4.3, Annex F. I: chapter 2.
