How technology can support

Technology can support candidate assessment, workforce flexibility, productivity and new operating concepts. This perspective identifies measures that depend on technical development or deployment.

Technology priorities

Final Report · §5.2 · Technical

The technology perspective identifies measures whose implementation depends on supporting technologies. Selecting Internal + Technology and Internal + External + Technology measures from the full catalogue gives 13 solutions.

These solutions do not necessarily require the development of entirely new technologies. In many cases, they rely on the deployment, integration, or wider adoption of existing technologies whose implementation remains limited, fragmented, or dependent on future development programmes. Consequently, this perspective is particularly relevant for technology providers, research programmes such as SESAR, and other actors involved in the evolution of the European ATM technological landscape.

The shortlist retains solutions addressing challenges classified as High Importance.

The resulting shortlist shows that the most relevant technical interventions are concentrated around four complementary themes: improving candidate assessment, increasing workforce flexibility through more flexible approaches, enhancing operational productivity through automation, and enabling new operational concepts through virtualisation and remote service provision.

Taken together, these findings suggest that the technical community's greatest contribution lies in increasing the productivity, flexibility, and scalability of the ATM system. While technology alone cannot solve workforce shortages, solutions such as advanced selection tools, more flexible licensing approaches, automation of workload-intensive tasks, and virtual service provision can significantly increase the effectiveness of the existing workforce and reduce pressure on the training process. As a result, technological development should be viewed as a key enabler of the long-term transformation of the European ATCO workforce system rather than as a standalone response to workforce shortages.
