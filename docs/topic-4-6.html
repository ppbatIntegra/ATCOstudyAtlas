The three competency domains

The workshop proposes three domains to distinguish what might transfer between settings and what may need further training and assessment.

Three domains, integrated performance

  • Core ATCO competence — common foundations of controller work.
  • Technical-system competence — competence with the relevant system and human–machine interface.
  • Operational-environment competence — competence for the relevant traffic, procedures and operating conditions.

The domains form a conceptual model, not an additive equation. Recognition still requires evidence of integrated performance under defined conditions.
