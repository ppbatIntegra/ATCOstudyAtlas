Objectives and scope

Final Report · §1.1, Objectives and scope

The primary objective of the study is to assess whether the current European ATCO licensing framework and workforce management practices can support future ATM operational, technological, and workforce requirements, and to identify potential improvements that could strengthen the long-term availability of qualified controllers across the European ATM network.

To achieve this objective, the study pursues four complementary goals:

  • To establish a common understanding of the current European ATCO workforce situation and the factors influencing controller availability;
  • To assess the regulatory, organisational, operational and training-related challenges affecting the ATCO management cycle;
  • To identify existing best practices and potential future practices capable of addressing those challenges; and
  • To develop evidence-based recommendations supporting the future evolution of the European ATCO licensing framework and workforce management practices.

The study covers the Member States of EASA and adopts a projection horizon extending to 2040. The assessment examines the full ATCO management cycle, including workforce planning, recruitment, selection, training, licensing, operational deployment and retention.

The study focuses on workforce availability and the factors influencing the relationship between future demand and supply of controllers. Economic considerations, including cost-benefit assessments of individual measures, fall outside the scope of the analysis.

A secondary objective of the study is to examine how the ATCO training and licensing scheme could be prepared for future changes in technology and new service delivery models, and to propose a possible way forward for future-proofing the framework.
