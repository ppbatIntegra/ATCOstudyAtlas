Missions

The study connects four objectives across the ATCO workforce, training and licensing system.

  • 01
    Understanding shortages

    Explain the drivers of workforce imbalance and explore how supply and demand may evolve.

  • 02
    Taking stock of existing practices

    Understand how recruitment, training, licensing and operational deployment work across different settings.

  • 03
    Looking for best practices and solutions

    Examine reported practices and potential measures, their prerequisites and their relevance to different actors.

  • 04
    Future-proofing the framework

    Explore how changing operations and automation could affect tasks, competence, training and licensing.
