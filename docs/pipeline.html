The ATCO Pipeline

Follow the complete workforce management cycle and see how its stages depend on one another.

  • 01
    Plan and attract

    Workforce planning, working conditions, recruitment and selection.

  • 02
    Train and qualify

    Initial training, unit training, assessment, licensing and sector qualifications.

  • 03
    Deploy and sustain

    Rostering, operational work, competence maintenance, retention and career transitions.

  • 04
    Learn and replan

    Use operational experience and performance analysis to improve the next cycle.

The pipeline is both an artefact to explore and a pathway connecting explanations across the Atlas.
