Recommendations for ANSPs

Final Report · Executive Summary · Recommendations
  • ANSPs should explore the root-cause analysis and the solution catalogue to identify practices applicable to them, identify the most effective local measures for closing the ATCO gap, and implement them as soon as practically possible.
  • ANSPs should also engage in regular, structured discussions around the topics addressed in the study to share experience, knowledge, and practices, and to ensure that the community can learn from individual efforts and ideas.
  • NSAs, ANSPs, PSOs and other state-level stakeholders involved in the planning of ANSP staffing and ATCO training and licensing should engage in regular, structured discussions around the topics addressed in the study and define ways of addressing the local ATCO gap as early as practically possible, and to ensure that all stakeholders share an understanding of the urgency of the issue, the underlying locally specific root causes, and the possible solutions.

Highest-priority measures

Final Report · §5.2 · ANSP perspective

Importance and feasibility criteria reduce the 78 solutions to 36 candidate recommendations from the ANSP perspective. Comparing these across the pillars identifies the measures with the greatest overall potential to reduce future shortages.

To do so, the candidate recommendations are first filtered based on the sensitivity analysis results. Priority is given to solutions affecting ATCO productivity and/or utilisation, as these variables were identified as the most influential controllable drivers of the ATCO gap. Among these, we prefer solutions that affect more than one model variable, as they can generate greater overall impact. This leaves four candidate recommendations. One of these additionally depends on enabling technologies and was therefore considered less feasible than the remaining measures.

Global top three — ANSP (operational) perspective (Source: Consortium assessment)

IDName
TL-UT.BP.1Dedicated management of instructor roles and workloads
TL-UT.FP.2Adopt competency-based progression models
TL-L.BP.2Facilitate mobility through common operational language arrangements and digital licence management

The three highest-ranked recommendations belong to Training & Licensing. They focus on strengthening the efficiency, capacity and flexibility of the training and licensing pipeline.

The result is also fully consistent with the preliminary qualitative assessment and the root-cause analysis. From the ANSP perspective, Unit Training emerged as the most critical area, while training capacity, training throughput, and workforce flexibility were repeatedly identified as the principal constraints affecting future controller availability. The prioritisation process therefore confirms that measures targeting these issues offer the greatest potential to reduce the future ATCO gap and strengthen the long-term resilience of the European ATCO workforce system.
