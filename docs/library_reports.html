Final Report

Final Report · Introduction, Structure of the document

To achieve these objectives, the report follows a structured analytical process that progressively builds the evidence base, assesses the challenges affecting controller availability and develops potential solutions and recommendations. The remainder of the report is structured as follows:

  • Section 2 describes the study methodology, data sources and analytical framework. It introduces the supply-demand model developed for the study, explains the principal model variables and documents the assumptions and limitations underpinning the quantitative analysis.
  • Section 3 presents the preliminary qualitative assessment based on the interviews and surveys conducted with participating ANSPs. It focuses on the ATCO pipeline used throughout the study and summarises the principal themes emerging from the consultation process, thereby contributing to the identification of the main factors affecting ATCO availability across the European ATM network.
  • Section 4 provides the root-cause analysis at the European level. It assesses the current and future ATCO workforce situation, examines the sensitivity of the supply-demand model, evaluates the relative importance of the different stages of the ATCO management cycle and assesses the challenges and solutions identified for each key area.
  • Section 5 presents the study's final set of recommended measures, building on combined evidence from quantitative modelling, stakeholder consultation, and solution assessment.
  • Section 6 summarises the main conclusions of the study, highlighting the principal findings, key messages and implications for the future evolution of the European ATCO licensing framework and workforce management practices.
  • Section 7 is dedicated to the secondary objective of future-proofing the ATCO training and licensing framework. It provides the overview of the analyses performed, and introduces the concept of Competency-based Licensing as the proposed way forward.

The report is complemented by a series of annexes providing supporting information, including records of stakeholder consultations, survey material, interview summaries, ANSP-level analyses and the detailed assessment of all identified solutions.

Intermediate Report

Intermediate Report · Overview and structure
  • The first chapter describes the data collection process, describes each data source and data table, and provides an overview of the findings from the data analysis.
  • The second chapter focuses on the logical model of the ATCO management cycle, which serves as the basis for further analysis, the framework for interpreting data, and for taking stock of the training and licensing practices of European ANSPs.
  • The third chapter provides a retrospective view of the evolution of ATCO shortages in Europe and identifies their root causes, utilising the capacity performance model and the logical model from chapter two.
  • The fourth chapter combines information from the data analysis and the logical model presented in the second chapter to develop a forecasting model and an outlook on the ATCO situation until 2040.
  • The fifth chapter provides an inventory of potential solutions that could be utilised to address future capacity shortfalls and ATCO shortages, based on the logical models and the outcomes of the data collection.
  • The sixth chapter provides a more detailed view of some cross-border initiatives among ANSPs from the perspective of ATCO training and licensing.

It is important to note that some parts of the study mentioned in this report are still works in progress, and so the corresponding sections will have to be revised and completed once the ANSP interviews have been conducted.
