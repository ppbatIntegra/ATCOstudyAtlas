Final stakeholder event · Slide 5

Study on the next generation ATCO licensing rules

DG MOVE initiated the study on the next generation ATCO licensing rules in December 2025. The study feeds into EASA rulemaking tasks 0668-5 and 0738.

The study provides a comprehensive inventory of current practices and conditions in the training and licensing of ATCOs, as well as a clear and transparent description of staff shortages. This is to establish a transparent, widely accepted baseline that underscores the sense of urgency around the topic.

The objective was to understand the root causes behind ATCO shortages, how solutions can be enabled, and the framework made future-proof, not necessarily leading to the introduction of new regulations,
