The model

Final Report · §2.2, Supply-demand model

To address this question, the study developed a supply-demand model that estimates how the ATCO workforce is expected to evolve over time and compares that future workforce with the number of controllers required to accommodate projected traffic demand. The model provides a common analytical framework through which hiring, licensing, training and operational factors can be assessed consistently across the study scope and translated into a common measure of workforce imbalance.

The purpose of the model is not limited to forecasting future staffing requirements. Equally importantly, it provides a structured way of understanding why ATCO shortages emerge, how they evolve over time and which factors are likely to have the greatest influence on future controller availability. This enables the study not only to quantify potential workforce shortages, but also to assess the extent to which different operational, workforce and training measures could contribute to reducing them. Challenges and solutions

Final Report · Chapter 5, Summary of Solutions and Recommendations

Once the full set of potential measures was identified and defined, the 78 candidate solutions were prioritised against two criteria to isolate those best able to reduce the future ATCO gap: the importance of the challenges addressed and the feasibility of implementation. The detailed prioritisation criteria are presented in Annex I.

Applying these criteria reduced the initial set of 78 solutions to a shortlist of 36 candidate recommendations. The full assessment is attached in Annex J. These constitute the starting point for the recommendations presented in this section per pillar and are subsequently assessed from different stakeholder perspectives.

Competency-based licensing

Final Report · Chapter 7, Our proposed solution for future-proofing

Combining everything we learned from the analyses, we developed the concept of Competency-Based Licensing (CBL), built on the three competence domains, and defined it as follows.

Competency-based licensing is an approach in which the scope of an ATCO’s licence privileges is explicitly linked to demonstrated competence across three complementary domains: core ATCO competencies, technical-system competence and operational-environment competence.
