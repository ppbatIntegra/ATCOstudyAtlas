# ATCO Knowledge Atlas reference register

Grouped inventory of the documents, datasets and project material cited in the study sources used for the Atlas. The register covers the draft final report’s nine bibliography entries and identifiable citations elsewhere in the baseline documents, including footnotes and annexes. Report series are grouped together. The source locators show where each item enters the study; edition or document details that the study does not establish are flagged separately.

## 8 References to resolve before publication

- **National and employer documents.** I §1.1.5 and F Table 2 refer to national legislation, implementing rules, guidance, collective agreements, employment and retirement frameworks, authority publications and ANSP reports. These are source families. The baseline documents do not give a document-by-document inventory or edition for each item.
- **Automation taxonomy.** W slide 6 uses a “Levels of Automation Taxonomy” as a conceptual reference without identifying a publication. T resolves the operational-task and ICAO competency sources; it does not establish the bibliography for the automation-level framework.
- **COOPANS EXODUS.** I §6.2 names the initiative but leaves the subsection for completion after interviews. No specific public deliverable is cited there.
- **Draft report cross-references.** F Table 1 marks *Performance Review* as `[2]`, and an Annex E utilisation note marks it `[8]`. In Annex A, the matching title is item **5**; item 2 is the intermediate report and item 8 is national performance plans. The register uses the title to identify the source and keeps the discrepancy visible.

Public publisher links are provided where identifiable. A link to a series or project page is labelled as such; it does not establish the exact edition used by the study. Internal deliverables, local files and interview evidence are listed as records without public download links.
