Challenges & Solutions

Connect a reported problem to possible responses, their dependencies and the actors who could implement them.

  • 01
    Understand the constraint

    Examine where a problem arises and the evidence supporting its explanation.

  • 02
    Inspect possible responses

    Explore measures affecting planning, hiring, training, licensing and operations.

  • 03
    Assess applicability

    Consider prerequisites, feasibility, time to effect and the limits of transferability.

  • 04
    Build a coordinated response

    Connect complementary measures and identify responsibilities and monitoring needs.

Reported practices and future proposals retain their source status. A practice’s inclusion in the catalogue does not itself demonstrate its effectiveness.
