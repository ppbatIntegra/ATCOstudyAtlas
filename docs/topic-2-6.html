How workforce supply evolves

Workforce supply evolves through inflows, outflows and training lead times. More training intake today is not the same as more qualified operational staff today.

Read the supply chain

  • Starting workforce provides the baseline.
  • Training intake, duration and success determine the timing and scale of new qualification.
  • Retirements and other attrition reduce the available workforce.
  • Transfers change local supply; they need not increase total European supply.

Transfers are shown here as a boundary consideration, not asserted to be an explicit variable in the final report model.

Source basis and evidence status

F: section 2.2 and Annex E. I: chapter 4 (earlier model version).
