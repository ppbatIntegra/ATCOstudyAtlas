Baseline data sources

Final Report · §2.1, Baseline data

Baseline data consist of publicly available European datasets, official publications and other reference sources used to establish a common and comparable evidence base for the study. These sources provide information on traffic demand, operational performance, workforce availability, training activity and workforce planning assumptions across the European ATM network and have been used for modelling purposes.

In addition to the European datasets presented above, the study uses a range of national and international reference sources throughout the different analytical activities undertaken. These sources were used to provide regulatory, operational and institutional context, support the interpretation and validation of findings and complement areas where equivalent harmonised European information is not available to support the parameterisation of specific variables.

Data sources and limitations

Intermediate Report · §1.1, Data sources

As part of its network functions, the EUROCONTROL Network Manager (NM) facilitates an annual collaborative decision-making (CDM) process to plan the operations of the European ATM network for the next 5 years, resulting in the Network Operations Plans (NOP). These plans are developed in close collaboration with all stakeholders and are based on a broad agreement, in line with CDM principles. The NOP details the traffic forecast on an ACC level, provides a forecast of ATFM delays, notes the key factors constraining capacity performance (if any), lists the capacity improvement measures planned by the ANSPs, and also those recommended by the NM experts, and provides data on the number of sectors that an ACC is able to open, sector-opening schemes and the evolution of ATCO numbers. Absolute ATCO numbers are only included as of the 2025 edition of the NOP; in previous editions, information was limited to changes.

Similar to the SES setup, Network Operations Plans also have follow-up documents which look at how the plans were realised and how the performance of the network evolved each year, called the Annual Network Operations Report (NOR) (the NM also published monthly Network Operations Reports, but for the purpose of the study, the annual editions are used). These reports provide a useful overview of network capacity performance and an inventory of ACC-level data on how the planned measures were realised and the key factors driving capacity performance.

We collected information from all editions of the Network Operations Plans and Reports available publicly on the EUROCONTROL website, including all NOP editions from 2017 to 2025 and all annual NORs from 2015 to 2024. Similar to the annual monitoring reports, we will update all relevant data tables once the 2025 edition of the annual NOR is published.

Given that figures on ATCO numbers and training are only introduced in detail in the most recent edition of the NOP, these sources are primarily used to provide insight into the reasons behind capacity shortages and the measures and solutions proposed by the NM.

EUROCONTROL ACE Benchmarking Report datasets

The Performance Review Committee of EUROCONTROL has been publishing detailed annual cost-effectiveness benchmarking reports of ANSPs since 2002. While these reports primarily compare various aspects of the cost-effectiveness and productivity of ANSPs and ATCOs, they also include a wide array of operational data that is useful for this study.

These datasets provide the longest continuous and most complete information on ATCO numbers for each ANSP and across all ATS units, the number of trainees in the ab initio and OJT phases, and detailed operational indicators useful for identifying key operational practices of the ANSPs. We extracted data from each edition of the report between 2002 and 2025 from table 05 ‘Total staff and ATCOs in OPS data’ of Annex 5, as well as operational data from table 07 ‘Operational data at ACC level’ of Annex 5 from the reports between 2011 and 2025 (as these are the years that could be correlated with the EUROCONTROL delay datasets).

The EUROCONTROL Aviation Intelligence Unit maintains datasets on ATFM delays in the European ATM network, covering both en route and airport arrival delays. These datasets provide daily figures for each entity in the dataset and include the following data:

  • The actual number of IFR movements (or arrivals).
  • The total ATFM delays generated on the day.
  • The breakdown of ATFM delays generated on the day under each delay reason code.
  • The number of IFR flights with delays longer than 15 minutes.

These data are provided either on the ANSP or airport level, depending on the dataset. Datasets are available in two versions: with or without post-operational adjustments. In the study, we use the datasets without the post-operational adjustments as these are better suited for operational analysis. The en route delay dataset covers 2011 to 2025 (including the early months of 2026), while the airport dataset covers only 2019 to 2025 (also including the early months of 2026).

The primary use of these datasets is to identify capacity shortages due to a lack of ATCOs and staffing problems, and to analyse the evolution of these shortfalls.

National regulations, publicly available policy and guidance documents

In addition to the data tables and sources detailed above, we also conducted an initial desk research to collect publicly available national legislative acts and regulatory documents concerning ATCO training, licensing and rostering. While these documents typically remain at a high level, some do set out parameters that constrain the day-to-day operations of the affected ANSPs. These legal acts and regulatory documents are compiled into comprehensive tables, which will be completed during the ANSP interviews to the extent possible.
