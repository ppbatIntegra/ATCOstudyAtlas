Sources supporting the analysis

Final Report · §2.1 · Baseline data

Baseline data consist of publicly available European datasets, official publications and other reference sources used to establish a common and comparable evidence base for the study. These sources provide information on traffic demand, operational performance, workforce availability, training activity and workforce planning assumptions across the European ATM network and have been used for modelling purposes.

Baseline data sources and information extracted (Source: Consortium assessment)

SourceInformation used in the study
EUROCONTROL
ACE Benchmarking		Historical workforce and operational data at ANSP and Area Control Centre (ACC) level, including number of ATCOs, flight hours controlled, controller hours on duty, ATCO productivity and trainees in different training phases. Used as the primary source for the historical workforce assessment.
EUROCONTROL
Performance Review		Traffic demand, operational performance and ATFM delay data, including delay attribution. Used to assess the relationship between workforce availability, operational capacity and network performance.
EUROCONTROL
STATFOR forecast		Low, base and high traffic growth scenarios used for future demand projections. Applied as growth trajectories to the latest available traffic demand data for each ACC.
EUROCONTROL
Aviation Outlook 2050		Extension of traffic growth assumptions beyond the STATFOR forecasting horizon to ensure consistency of traffic projections up to 2036.
RP3
Performance Plan		Workforce planning assumptions, training intakes and training outputs used to support the workforce model where equivalent ANSP-specific information is not available.
RP4
Performance Plan		Updated workforce planning assumptions, training intakes and training outputs used to support the workforce model where equivalent ANSP-specific information is not available.

In addition to the European datasets presented above, the study uses a range of national and international reference sources throughout the different analytical activities undertaken. These sources were used to provide regulatory, operational and institutional context, support the interpretation and validation of findings and complement areas where equivalent harmonised European information is not available to support the parameterisation of specific variables.

Where information is not available through harmonised European datasets, additional reference sources and information obtained directly from ANSPs are used to complement the assessment.

Questions and analytical scope

Intermediate Report · §1.2 · Analytical process

The main data-analysis scope was 2018–2025. Longer time series were used to reveal strategic trends and historical context.

The data analysis focused on gathering information on the following key questions:

  • How did ATCO numbers evolve in the past, and did ANSPs have sufficient ATCO staffing to cope with traffic demand? How did ATCO utilisation and productivity evolve?
  • What were the reasons behind capacity shortages, and how much did these relate to ATCO staffing?
  • How do the key parameters of ATCO training and licensing compare across ANSPs? Are there any direct correlations visible between training and licensing practices and capacity shortages?
