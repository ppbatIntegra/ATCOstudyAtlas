Interviews and survey

Final Report · §2.1, Interviews and survey

Interviews were conducted with ANSPs across the study scope to obtain qualitative evidence on the factors influencing ATCO availability that cannot be derived from publicly available datasets alone. The objective was to capture operational, organisational, training and regulatory perspectives relating to the management of the ATCO workforce and to provide context for the trends identified through the quantitative assessment.

Semi-structured interviews were conducted using a common interview guide to ensure consistency across participating organisations. The interviews were carried out through a combination of in-person and online meetings, depending on the circumstances and availability of each ANSP. The consultation covered workforce planning, recruitment, selection, training, licensing, operational deployment, workforce flexibility and future developments affecting the ATCO management cycle.

The interview programme included 17 of the 30 ANSPs within the scope of the study, representing approximately 57% of the target population. Whilst the participation rate does not cover the entire study scope, the sample was selected and completed in a way that ensures representation across the principal types of ANSPs operating within the SES. A dedicated survey was distributed to ANSPs across the study scope to collect standardised ANSP-specific information that is not consistently available through publicly accessible sources. The survey complemented the baseline datasets and interview programme by gathering information on workforce planning, recruitment, selection, training, licensing, workforce flexibility and other factors influencing ATCO availability across the ATCO management cycle.

The questionnaire was developed by the consortium based on the information requirements identified during the study and subsequently reviewed and validated by both the European Commission and the Project Steering Group to ensure its relevance, completeness and alignment with the objectives of the study. The complete survey questionnaire, together with a summary of the responses, is provided in Annex E and the evidence collected through the survey is presented in Section 3.2.

The survey remained open until 31 July 2026 (inclusive). Responses were received from 26 of the 30 ANSPs within the scope of the study, representing a participation rate exceeding 85% and providing broad coverage of the European ATM network. Information on the ANSPs invited to participate, respondent organisations and overall participation statistics is provided in Annex C.

Aggregate consultation findings

Final Report · Chapter 3, Interview and survey analyses

Interviews constituted one of the principal sources of qualitative evidence used throughout the study. A total of 17 ANSPs participated in the interview programme, representing a broad range of operational environments, organisational models and geographical contexts across the European ATM network. The interviews were used to complement the quantitative analysis by providing a deeper understanding of the organisational, operational and regulatory factors influencing ATCO availability. The complete list of participating organisations, including interview dates, is provided in Annex C.

Although substantial differences exist across ANSPs, the interviews revealed both common challenges and divergent approaches to hiring, training, licensing and operational deployment. The following sections summarise the main findings identified through the interview analysis. A summary of each ANSP interviewed can be found in Annex D.

The survey provided a quantitative complement to the stakeholder interviews, enabling the capture of common patterns and the quantification of the prevalence of specific challenges, practices and expectations across participating ANSPs. Responses were received from 26 ANSPs, covering more than 85% and representing a broad range of operational environments, organisational models and traffic contexts. Information on the ANSPs invited to participate, respondent organisations and overall participation statistics is provided in Annex C, and the full set of survey questions and the summary of the responses, individually per question, is provided in Annex E.

The findings presented in this section are structured around the four analytical pillars used throughout the study. The objective of this section is to present the main trends emerging from the survey and to establish a quantitative evidence base for the subsequent root-cause analysis. Rather than identifying causes or recommending solutions, the section highlights areas of convergence and divergence across ANSPs regarding workforce planning, training and licensing practices, operational arrangements, and future adaptation needs.
