Taking stock of practice

Final Report · §1.1 and §2.1 · Objectives and input information
  • To assess the regulatory, organisational, operational and training-related challenges affecting the ATCO management cycle;

The assessment relies on multiple complementary sources of evidence to capture both the quantitative and qualitative dimensions of ATCO availability across the European ATM network. These sources provide the information required to assess the current workforce situation, identify the factors influencing controller availability and support the development of the European supply-demand model and the ATCO management cycle assessment.

The study draws on three principal sources of information.

  • Baseline data, comprising publicly available datasets and official publications providing harmonised information on traffic demand, operational performance, workforce indicators and training activity across the European ATM network;
  • Survey distributed to ANSPs across the study scope, providing standardised ANSP-specific information on the different stages of the ATCO management cycle; and
  • Interviews conducted with several ANSPs, providing qualitative evidence and operational insights on workforce planning, recruitment, training, licensing and workforce management practices.

Each source contributes a different perspective to the assessment. Publicly available data provides consistency and comparability at European level, whilst information collected directly from ANSPs provides the operational detail required to understand the organisational and training-related factors influencing workforce availability. Together, these sources provide the evidence base used throughout the study.

A common lifecycle framework

Intermediate Report · Chapter 2 · Management cycle

The ATCO pipeline describes the steps and decisions through which ANSPs plan, develop and manage controller resources.

The pipeline focuses on management processes, linking workforce planning to recruitment, training, qualification, operational deployment and feedback.

The pipeline provides a common framework for comparing ANSP practices, considering regulatory constraints and identifying where decisions at one stage affect later outcomes.
