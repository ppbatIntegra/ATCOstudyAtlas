Licensing and recognition options

Training and assessment establish evidence of competence. Formal recognition asks what that evidence authorises, in which conditions, and with what boundaries.

Keep the concepts distinct

  • CBTA concerns training and assessment.
  • Proposed competency-based licensing concerns the basis for formal recognition.
  • System-based licensing and non-geographic validation are related options requiring their own definitions.
  • Cross-border service provision is an operational arrangement, not a synonym for a licensing model.

These are proposals for exploration. Regulatory records require verification against the instruments before publication.
